# Adidas Sales Performance Analysis

A sales analysis and dashboard built from Adidas US sales data. This was the capstone project for the BeTechified Data Analytics Beginners course (Group 4C).

## Project overview
The goal was to understand where Adidas sales and profit come from by region, retailer, product and sales method, and how sales change through the year. The results are shown in a one-page dashboard.

## Dataset
- 9,648 sales records, January 2020 to December 2021
- 13 columns: Retailer, Retailer ID, Invoice Date, Region, State, City, Product, Price per Unit, Units Sold, Total Sales, Operating Profit, Operating Margin, Sales Method
- 6 retailers, 5 regions, 50 states, 6 product categories, 3 sales methods (in-store, outlet, online)

## Tools
Spreadsheet (.xlsx file) with pivot tables and charts.

## What is in the workbook
- **Data Sales Adidas**: the raw sales data
- **Pivot Table 1**: sales and profit by product
- **Pivot Table 2**: sales and profit by sales method
- **Pivot Table 3**: sales trend by month
- **Dashboard**: summary numbers and five charts (sales by region, sales by retailer, sales vs profit by product, sales vs profit by sales method, sales trend by month)

## Key numbers
- Total revenue: $899.9 million
- Total units sold: 2,478,861
- Total operating profit: $332.1 million (about 36.9% of revenue)
- Average price per unit: $45.22

## Key findings
1. **West is the top region** with $269.9M in sales (30% of the total). Midwest is the lowest with $135.8M (15%).
2. **West Gear is the top retailer** with $243.0M (27%), followed by Foot Locker ($220.1M) and Sports Direct ($182.5M). Walmart is the lowest with $74.6M.
3. **Men's Street Footwear is the top product** with $208.8M in sales and the best profit margin of all products (about 39.7%).
4. **In-store sales bring the most revenue** ($356.6M, 40%), but **online sells the most units** (939,093) and has the highest profit margin (39.0%).
5. **Sales peak in July ($95.5M) and August ($92.2M)** and are lowest in March ($56.8M). Months are combined across 2020 and 2021.
